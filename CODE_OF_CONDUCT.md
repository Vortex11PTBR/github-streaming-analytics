# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

Our community is dedicated to providing a harassment-free experience for everyone.

Be respectful and kind. Harassment, discrimination, or abusive behaviour will not be tolerated.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, please open an issue or contact the maintainers.

For the full text see: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
